# Community conduct

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, explain technical disagreements with evidence, and give actionable feedback about the work. Harassment, discriminatory behavior, threats, deliberate privacy violations and publishing another person's confidential information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may moderate discussions and restrict participation to protect contributors and the project. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a channel already known to you; do not post sensitive details publicly. Do not invent a contact address or publish private information while seeking assistance.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests and project-maintained discussion spaces. Technical decisions remain reviewable on their merits. Enforcement is handled by the repository maintainer; no independent review committee or guaranteed response time is asserted.
